Oscar Castellino — pictures folder
=================================

Press / performance images for oscarcastellino.com.

Naming conventions (recent productions)
---------------------------------------
- Così fan tutte (Barefoot Opera / Grimeborn):
  "Cosi fan tutte Barefoot Opera - Oscar Castellino as Guglielmo - [scene] - credit @areyoutakingthepics.jpg"
  Role: Guglielmo. Always keep the @areyoutakingthepics credit in the filename.

- The Barber of Seville (2023):
  "Barber of Seville 2023 - Oscar Castellino as Figaro - [scene].jpg"
  Role: Figaro.

Gallery catalogue
-----------------
All Oscar images (with titles, production, role, year, credit) are listed in
../gallery-data.json and shown on gallery.html (full) and index.html#gallery (preview).

Downloads require acknowledging photographer credit before the file is saved.

Non-Oscar files kept here (collaborator portraits, posters) are excluded from
the public press gallery catalogue.
