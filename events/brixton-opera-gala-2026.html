The Grand Brixton Opera Gala brings together the Brixton Chamber Orchestra and the celebrated Pegasus Opera Company for two unmissable open-air performances. International baritone Oscar Castellino takes centre stage as a featured soloist, performing some of opera's most thrilling and beloved music.

From the comic genius of Rossini's Barber of Seville to the champagne-fizz of Strauss's Die Fledermaus and the soaring drama of Puccini — the programme is a grand tour through opera's greatest moments, performed for free in two of South London's most beautiful outdoor settings.

Oscar Castellino Performs

Programme Highlights

Rossini, Strauss and Puccini — three composers, three worlds, one unforgettable evening

Gioachino Rossini

Largo al factotum

The Barber of Seville — Figaro's Cavatina, Act I

Perhaps the most famous baritone showpiece in all of opera, Figaro's breathless self-introduction is a whirlwind of patter, wit and vocal fireworks. "Largo al factotum della città" — Make way for the factotum of the city! — Oscar commands the stage as the irresistible barber whose services everyone in Seville depends upon. A virtuosic tour-de-force demanding extraordinary agility, comic timing and sheer vocal exuberance.

Gioachino Rossini

Dunque io son

The Barber of Seville — Figaro & Rosina Duet, Act I

In this sparkling duet, the scheming Figaro shares the Count's secret with a gleeful Rosina — both rejoicing in a conspiracy against her pompous guardian. Full of playful exchanges and irresistible Rossinian bubbling energy, Dunque io son showcases Oscar's comic flair and the radiant chemistry between baritone and soprano. The audience is drawn into Figaro and Rosina's delicious plot with every note.

Johann Strauss II

Brüderlein und Schwesterlein

Die Fledermaus — The Champagne Drinking Song, Act II

The heart of Strauss's effervescent Die Fledermaus, this famous drinking song rings out at Prince Orlofsky's extravagant masked ball as the revellers abandon all formality and toast to eternal brotherhood and sisterhood. Oscar's warm baritone leads the charge in one of opera's most jubilant and audience-rousing moments — the perfect number for an open-air crowd to join in the spirit of the occasion.

Giacomo Puccini

A Selection from Puccini

Full programme to be announced

The full gala programme also features music from Puccini — the master of operatic emotion whose soaring melodies have captivated audiences for over a century. The 55-piece Brixton Chamber Orchestra will bring his sweeping orchestral writing to life under the open sky, with dramatic works including music from Turandot.

Co-Presenter

Pegasus Opera Company

Pegasus Opera Company is one of Britain's most celebrated professional opera companies, with a history spanning more than thirty years of outstanding opera performance. Founded with a mission to champion artists from global majority heritage backgrounds, Pegasus has brought world-class opera to audiences across London and beyond.

The company has built a remarkable reputation for artistic excellence combined with genuine community engagement — producing full opera productions, concert performances and education programmes that bring the transformative power of opera to everyone, regardless of background.

Their annual partnership with the Brixton Chamber Orchestra for the Opera Gala has become a highlight of the South London cultural calendar, drawing audiences of all ages to enjoy free, world-class opera in the open air.

The Opera Gala — Brockwell Park

An open-air afternoon of world-class opera in one of South London's most beloved parks — free entry, all welcome

🕐 Sunday 23 August 2026
🏗 Brockwell Park, London SE24
🎪 Concert from 3:00pm
✓ Free Entry

Join Pegasus Opera Company and the Brixton Chamber Orchestra for an unforgettable afternoon of opera at Brockwell Park. On Sunday 23 August 2026, the park becomes the stage for a spectacular open-air performance featuring an array of operatic masterworks — soaring arias, beloved duets and orchestral highlights performed by world-class singers and a full 55-piece orchestra.

Bring a blanket, bring a picnic, and enjoy a free afternoon of opera under the open sky with friends and family. Whether you are a lifelong opera lover or coming for the first time, this is a joyful, inclusive event for everyone.

Oscar Castellino performs alongside fellow Pegasus Opera Company soloists in an exciting programme featuring music by Rossini, Strauss and Puccini. The Brixton Chamber Orchestra provides the musical backdrop for this South London highlight.

For the most up-to-date information about the Brockwell Park performance, including any access arrangements or programme changes, visit the Pegasus Opera Company website.
