The Production

Undercover. Exposed. Still urgent.

Disguised as a Turkish migrant worker in West Germany during the 1980s, journalist Günter Wallraff entered the hidden world of migrant labour for two years — experiencing and exposing exploitation, racism and harsh working conditions. His revelations shocked Europe.

Featuring music by renowned Turkish composer Tuluğ Tırpan, this docu-opera explores the everyday encounters and ethics of Wallraff’s undercover work, considering questions of cultural appropriation through a contemporary lens and investigating what drove Wallraff’s transformation.

“Few people have done more to change the way postwar Germany looks at itself” — The Guardian, on Günter Wallraff
“Relentlessly contemporary… Mehmet Ergen carefully reconstructs the stages of Wallraff’s undercover investigation — every scene hits its mark” — Süddeutsche Zeitung, on the 2025 Kunstfest Weimar production
